1Build the structure

Three sections, nothing more: opening balance, cash in, cash out. Closing feeds next week's opening. If updating this takes more than 30 minutes a week, you've built too much.

2Populate with real data

Weeks 1–4: actual opening balance, inflows placed in the week the money realistically lands (not the week the order was placed), committed outflows. Weeks 5–13: directional, following your normal monthly pattern.

3Make it a Monday habit

Set a recurring 8:00 AM Monday reminder. Fifteen minutes: update the opening balance, adjust any cash-in that did not land as expected, roll the view forward one week. The calendar event matters more than the formula.

"Now I'm not reacting to the bank account. I'm managing it."

Before any significant financial decision, look at the view first. Can you afford it that specific week, without creating a problem?
